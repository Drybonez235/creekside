# 9 Meta Ads Case Studies With Real Numbers: 20x ROAS, $977K Revenue, and 47% Lower CPI

Meta Ads case studies usually come in two flavors: screenshots with no context, or long stories with no numbers. Neither helps you figure out what your account should actually be producing.

This roundup includes 9 real Meta Ads engagements with verified numbers from Ads Manager: revenue, ROAS, cost per acquisition, and the strategy behind each result. They span high-ticket dental, B2B SaaS, e-commerce, meal prep, education, and mobile apps.

Each entry links to the full case study with the complete breakdown.

A high-ticket dental implant practice was self-managing $30K/month in Meta spend with no attribution. Creekside rebuilt the account, added Salesforce attribution and lead qualification, and produced $977K in attributed revenue over 90 days from 2,543 leads.

## What These 9 Meta Ads Case Studies Have in Common

Three patterns show up in every winning account:

- **Systematic creative testing beats one-off ads.** The B2B SaaS, retail, and app accounts all run structured rotation systems: classify every ad, cut losers on a fixed window, keep proven winners live.
- **Optimize on the event that makes money.** The $977K dental result and the 417 B2B demos both came from feeding the real conversion event (qualified lead, booked demo) back to Meta instead of optimizing for cheap form fills.
- **New-customer discipline.** The meal prep brands exclude past purchasers entirely, so reported ROAS reflects actual growth, not retargeting inflation.
