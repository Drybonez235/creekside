## What These 15 Google Ads Case Studies Have in Common

Across all 15 accounts, the same three variables separated strong performance from wasted spend:

- **Conversion tracking that measures the real business outcome.** The GLP-1 account's turnaround started with fixing duplicate tracking. Several accounts were optimizing toward events that did not matter.
- **High-intent keyword structure.** Every dental, legal, and home services win came from concentrating budget on procedure-specific, locally targeted, commercial-intent searches instead of broad terms.
- **Ongoing optimization, not set-and-forget.** The accounts that hit $2-$4 CPAs got there over months of iteration, not at launch.
