On January 16, 2026, ChatGPT released news that ChatGPT ads will begin appearing inside its free and go tiers within the next few months. Within days of that announcement, a wave of consultants and freelancers appeared positioning themselves as AI advertising specialists ready to help businesses get ahead of this new platform.

Here is the problem with that framing: you cannot be an expert in something that was just announced. No one has real, hands-on experience running ads inside ChatGPT because the ads do not exist yet. What is being sold right now is theory dressed up as expertise.

What businesses actually need is not an AI guru with a presentation full of prompts and speculation. They need someone who has been running ads alongside AI-generated content, inside a platform that has already tested this exact model. That is a Google Ads manager.

This is not hypothetical. Google and Gemini have been running that experiment for months.

## ChatGPT Ads Are Not the First Time AI Has Carried Paid Placements. Google Already Did This.

Google and Gemini have already started experimenting with showing ads inside their AI platform. Sponsored results currently appear right above the AI overview in search results, meaning when a user receives an AI-generated summary, there are paid ad placements sitting above it. This is live, not a concept being tested somewhere in a lab.

According to Creekside Marketing's analysis, Google is also using data from users' Gemini conversations to influence who gets shown ads and who is most likely to convert. The data pipeline from AI interaction to ad targeting is already built and running on Google's platform today.

ChatGPT's move is not a surprise or an innovation in that sense. It is a logical step. When you are entering a market where the dominant competitor monetizes through search advertising and already runs ads next to AI summaries, you study and replicate that model. ChatGPT will follow Google's lead because Google's approach is the proven one, and it is the approach that produces revenue at scale.

This matters for one practical reason: Google Ads managers already have hands-on experience running campaigns that appear alongside AI-generated content and being targeted to users based on AI platform behavior. That experience does not exist anywhere else yet.

## Why Anyone Calling Themselves a "ChatGPT Ads Expert" Right Now Is Selling You Theory

Google Ads managers, not AI consultants, are best positioned to run ChatGPT ads because the underlying mechanics of AI-adjacent advertising are already built and tested on Google. Expertise in this space requires hands-on experience with real campaigns, not theoretical knowledge of how large language models work.

This is a self-serving point, and it is worth naming that upfront. But the logic holds regardless of who makes it.

When ChatGPT ads go live, there will be no shortage of consultants, freelancers, and agencies positioning themselves as specialists. They will show you prompts, frameworks, and research. They will claim they have been studying the ChatGPT platform and have a methodology ready.

What they will not have is the thing that actually matters: real campaign experience.

Expertise in advertising on AI platforms is not about understanding how the AI generates responses. It is about understanding bidding mechanics, audience signal quality, conversion data feedback loops, and how ad platforms use engagement behavior to optimize delivery over time. These are skills Google Ads managers have been building for years on a platform that already runs ads adjacent to AI output.

The skill transfer from Google Ads to ChatGPT ads is direct. The skill transfer from studying AI to ChatGPT ads is largely theoretical. When CPCs are low and the algorithm is still learning during the first few months, the difference between those two starting points matters enormously.

## The CPC Opportunity Window Is Real and It Will Not Stay Open Long

Early movers on ChatGPT ads will pay significantly lower CPCs than businesses that wait. New ad platforms price inventory at a discount in the early period because they need to give advertisers a concrete reason to shift budget away from Google and Facebook. ChatGPT is no exception to that pattern.

According to Creekside Marketing's analysis, ChatGPT will need to sell ad placements at more of a discount in the early period because the platform is new and competing against systems with proven return on investment. At the same time, user demand on ChatGPT will be substantial. That combination of high user volume and low advertiser competition is what produces cheap early CPCs on new platforms.

The window closes as more advertisers enter. When competitors in your category start bidding on ChatGPT inventory, CPCs normalize and the early mover advantage disappears. This is the same dynamic that played out on Facebook Ads, YouTube Ads, and every other platform that launched with new inventory and took time for advertiser demand to catch up.

For newer businesses in particular, this window matters more than it does for established players. Established businesses move more slowly on new platforms because they have more internal processes and more risk tolerance required before allocating budget to something unproven. A leaner, faster-moving business can enter categories where established competitors are not yet bidding and build audience data at a fraction of what it will cost later.

## How to Prepare Your Strategy Before ChatGPT Ads Go Live

Replicate what is already working on Google. That is the most direct preparation strategy, and it comes directly from the fact that Google has already built and tested the AI ad model that ChatGPT will follow.

Google is already providing data on how ads perform alongside AI summaries and AI overviews. If your business is currently running Google Ads and appearing in those placements, you have a data source that most ChatGPT advertisers will not have when the platform launches. Your conversion rates, cost per lead, and audience signals from Google represent the closest real-world proxy for what ChatGPT ad performance will look like out of the gate.

According to Creekside Marketing's analysis of accounts managing over $20 million in ad spend, the three steps that position a business most effectively for ChatGPT ads are:

**Get on Google Ads now if you are not already running them.** The campaigns and data you generate from running alongside Google's AI overviews are your best baseline for ChatGPT. You enter the new platform with conversion benchmarks and audience insights rather than starting from zero with no comparison point.

**Commit to testing for at least a couple of months after ChatGPT ads launch.** The algorithm will shift significantly in its early period, especially as competitors enter specific categories. Businesses that expect immediate results and pull budget after a few weeks will repeat the most expensive part of any new platform (the early learning months) over and over, never reaching the period where data compounds into consistent returns. This is the same dynamic that kills Google Ads accounts when businesses switch agencies too quickly. For more on that pattern, see our breakdown of [why Google Ads ROI compounds over time rather than delivering instant results](/blog/stop-expecting-vending-machine-google-ads-roi/).

**Use your existing Google Ads playbook as the starting hypothesis.** Because ChatGPT is building its advertising system on the model Google already proved, the campaigns, offers, and messaging that perform on Google are your best first guess for what will perform on ChatGPT. You are not starting from scratch. You are adapting what already works to new inventory.

## What This Means If Your Google Ads Are Not Performing Well Right Now

ChatGPT ads are not a shortcut around a weak Google Ads foundation. If your current campaigns are not generating profitable leads, a new and cheaper ad channel will not fix the underlying problem. The fundamentals that matter on Google (clean conversion data, a defined cost per acquisition target, offer clarity) matter on every platform.

The businesses best positioned to take advantage of ChatGPT ads are the ones with solid Google Ads accounts today: clear conversion tracking, a defined cost per lead, and campaigns generating real revenue rather than just form completions. Those businesses can adapt their approach to ChatGPT ads with a meaningful starting point and historical benchmarks to test against.

Businesses without that foundation will face the same problems on a new platform that has even less data to help them debug what is going wrong.

This is why we focus on getting Google Ads fundamentals right before expanding to additional channels. The discipline required to run profitable search campaigns transfers directly to every new platform. A messy account does not become a clean one because the CPCs are cheaper.

The most common issue we find in audits is accounts optimizing for the wrong conversion signal: tracking form completions as conversions and receiving unqualified leads at scale, or running match types without the CRM data needed to tell the algorithm what a real conversion looks like. These problems follow you to new platforms. ## Frequently Asked Questions About ChatGPT Ads

**When will ChatGPT ads actually go live?**

ChatGPT announced on January 16, 2026 that ads are coming to its free and go tiers within the next few months from that date. The exact launch date was not specified in the announcement. New platform rollouts often shift on timing, but the direction is confirmed and the infrastructure investment is real.

**Will ChatGPT ads work for local service businesses?**

It is too early to have definitive performance data because the ads are not live yet. Based on how Google's AI overview ads perform, businesses with clear commercial intent signals should see relevant traffic from AI platforms. Local service businesses should plan to gather data for at least two months before drawing conclusions about what works.

**Should I stop running Google Ads to allocate budget to ChatGPT ads?**

No. ChatGPT ads are an additional inventory source, not a replacement for Google. Google has significantly more user behavior data, commercial intent signals, and algorithmic refinement built up over decades. Keep your Google Ads running and treat ChatGPT as an expansion channel once the platform launches and your foundation is solid.

**Do I need to hire an AI specialist or a Google Ads manager to run ChatGPT ads?**

A Google Ads manager. The underlying mechanics of AI-adjacent advertising are already being practiced on Google's platform. Someone with hands-on experience running campaigns that appear alongside AI overviews and targeting audiences based on AI interaction data has a direct and applicable skill set. Someone who studies AI models in theory does not have that hands-on advantage yet.

**How different will ChatGPT ad targeting be from Google Ads targeting?**

Google uses Gemini conversation data to influence who sees ads and who is most likely to convert. ChatGPT will likely follow a similar model, using conversation signals to identify user intent and buying behavior. The data infrastructure will be less mature at launch because ChatGPT does not have Google's decades of behavioral data. Targeting will improve substantially over the first year as the platform accumulates more signal.
