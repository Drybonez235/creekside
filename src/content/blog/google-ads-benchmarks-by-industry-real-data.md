## Home Services Google Ads Benchmarks

According to Creekside Marketing's home services campaign data, Google Ads cost $3-$25 per click depending on service urgency. Emergency services like HVAC repair command the highest CPCs ($15-$30) because buyers need someone immediately. Recurring services like lawn care and pest control sit at the low end ($3-$10).

**CPC by service category:**

| Service Type | CPC Range | Intent Level |
|-------------|-----------|-------------|
| Emergency (plumbing, HVAC, electrical) | $15 - $30 | Immediate |
| Installation (paving, roofing, awnings) | $8 - $20 | Project |
| Recurring (lawn care, pest control, cleaning) | $3 - $10 | Maintenance |

**What this looks like in real campaigns:** A Virginia asphalt contractor (Perfect Parking) ran at $12-$15 per click and generated 32 leads in 12 days at $127 cost per lead on an $8,000-$9,000/month budget. Landmark Lawn and Landscape generated 1,500+ conversions at $50.35 cost per conversion over two years, funding a franchise expansion into Atlanta. Green Shield Pest Solutions achieved 79 conversions at $92.60 per lead, then reduced that cost by 19% through search term management.

The consistent structural pattern across high-performing home service accounts: tight geographic targeting, 2-3 keywords per ad group maximum, and aggressive negative keyword management.

## How to Use These Benchmarks

These numbers are reference points, not guarantees. Every campaign's performance depends on market competition, landing page quality, conversion tracking accuracy, and account structure. Here is what to do with this data:

**If your numbers are worse than these ranges,** the problem is almost certainly structural: wrong campaign type, poor keyword targeting, broken conversion tracking, or a landing page that does not convert. Start with an [account audit](/digital-advertising/google-ads/).

**If your numbers fall within these ranges,** your account is performing at market rate. Improvement comes from tightening negatives, improving landing pages, and layering audience signals.

**If your numbers are better than these ranges,** you are likely leaving scale on the table. Increasing budget at current efficiency is the highest-leverage move.

## FAQ

### How often is this benchmark data updated?

Creekside Marketing updates these benchmarks quarterly based on active campaign performance data. The lastModified date in the page metadata reflects the most recent update. Benchmark ranges shift as Google's auction dynamics, competitor density, and platform features change.

### Where does this data come from?

Every number on this page comes from campaigns Creekside Marketing actively manages. We do not use industry surveys, Google's suggested benchmarks, or third-party aggregation tools. The data source is our own campaign dashboards across $20M+ in total managed ad spend.

### Why are the CPC ranges so wide within each industry?

CPC ranges reflect real variation within each vertical. A lawn care company in a mid-size Tennessee market competes in a completely different auction than an HVAC emergency contractor in Phoenix. Service type, geographic competition, keyword intent, and time of year all move CPC within the stated ranges.

### Can I use these benchmarks to set my Google Ads budget?

Yes. Find your industry row, multiply the midpoint CPC by your target monthly clicks, and add 20% for testing overhead. For a more precise estimate based on your specific market and service, [contact Creekside Marketing](/contact/) for a campaign-specific projection.

### How do these compare to industry averages from WordStream or Google?

Third-party benchmark reports aggregate self-reported data across all skill levels and account structures. Our data comes exclusively from professionally managed accounts with proper conversion tracking, negative keyword hygiene, and optimized bidding strategies. Our conversion rates tend to run higher and CPAs lower than industry averages because the accounts are actively managed.
