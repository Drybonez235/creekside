    sectionLabel: "The Strategy"
    heading: "Dual-Objective Architecture with a Creative Rotation System"
    paragraphs:
      - "We built a campaign structure that separated the two objectives from the start. Seventy percent of the $7,500 monthly budget went to core French Fitness brand campaigns and showroom visit campaigns, the proven converters. The remaining 30% tested product-specific campaigns and new audience expansions."
      - "Underlying the structure was a full account rebuild: pixel events properly tracking both e-commerce purchases and showroom visit intent, audience segments separated by intent layer, and creative testing frameworks that could tell us which products and which formats were driving which outcomes."
      - "The more durable strategic decision was solving creative fatigue before it became a crisis. Fitness equipment ads burn through creative fast. The same product images stop converting after a few weeks of consistent exposure: frequency climbs, CTR drops, and ROAS follows. We worked with Tim (the client's content lead) to build a monthly rotation system using showroom video footage of machines being demonstrated. Fresh creative kept quality scores high and prevented the ROAS decay that kills most retail Meta accounts over time."
