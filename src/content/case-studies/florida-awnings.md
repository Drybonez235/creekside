      - "Florida Awnings operated three distinct service entities across the state: Central Florida Awning LLC at $3,000 per month, Southeast Awnings at $4,200 per month, and a Gulf Coast expansion entity. Google Ads ran high-intent search campaigns for homeowners actively looking for awning installation, patio covers, and outdoor living solutions in each area. Meta Ads ran visual creative campaigns to build awareness and capture homeowners earlier in the decision process."

The goal was not to find one winning approach and apply it everywhere. The goal was to find what worked in each territory and optimize independently."
