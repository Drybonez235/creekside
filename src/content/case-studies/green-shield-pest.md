      - "We deployed a pest control campaign framework that Creekside has refined across multiple pest control clients. The core principles: standardized landing pages optimized for phone calls and form submissions, phrase match keywords with a maximum of 2-3 per ad group, geographic targeting within a strict 10-mile service radius, and budget allocated 80% to general pest control with 20% to specialty services."
      - "High-performing ad copy was pulled from proven campaigns and localized for Green Shield's specific market and services. Continuous search term analysis identified and added negatives weekly, ensuring spend stayed focused on buyers rather than researchers. Every dollar of budget was pointed at searches where someone had a problem and needed it solved today."
      - "The tight radius targeting eliminated wasted impressions from searchers outside the service area. The low keyword count per ad group kept quality scores high and CPCs competitive. Together, these created a campaign structure where efficiency compounds over time rather than degrading as the account matures."
