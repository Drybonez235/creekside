    sectionLabel: "The Strategy"
    heading: "Precision Targeting on a Tight Budget"
    paragraphs:
      - "The first move was structural: remove the expensive mismatched keywords (dental implants, veneers, cosmetic terms) that were sending high-cost clicks to pages that could not convert them. Every keyword in the account needed to match the landing page it pointed to and the patient the practice was actually trying to reach."
      - "We rebuilt around high purchase-intent local searches for general dentistry, specifically patients looking for a dentist near them who was accepting new patients, covered specific insurances, or offered same-day appointments. We leveraged specialized optimization tools within Google Ads to accelerate quality score improvements and bid efficiency, and we wrote ad copy designed for high click-through rates in a competitive local market."
      - "As the account matured and budget allowed, campaign structure was expanded to break out service types. But the core principle never changed: every dollar had to point to a keyword that matched an ad that matched a landing page that matched what a patient was actually looking for."
