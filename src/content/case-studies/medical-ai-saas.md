  - type: "text"
    sectionLabel: "The Strategy"
    heading: "A Creative Testing Engine, Not One-Off Ads"
    paragraphs:
      - "We rebuilt the account around a disciplined testing system. Every ad gets classified red, yellow, or green based on performance. Underperformers are cut inside a 12-14 day window, and proven winners keep running, with 9-10 winning ads live concurrently at any time. New creative enters the rotation on a fixed cadence instead of ad hoc."
      - "On the targeting side, campaigns were segmented by medical specialty so messaging could speak directly to each audience's workflow. And instead of optimizing for clicks or cheap leads, we wired the booked-demo event into Meta through the Conversions API, so the algorithm optimized on the one action that actually matters to the business."
