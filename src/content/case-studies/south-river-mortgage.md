      - "South River Mortgage is one of the five largest reverse mortgage providers in the United States, operating across 39 states.   - type: "text"
    sectionLabel: "The Strategy"
    heading: "A Multi-Stage Funnel Built Around Lead Quality, Not Volume"
    paragraphs:
      - "The single most important decision we made was how we defined success. Instead of optimizing for form submissions or raw lead volume, we built the entire campaign around pricing qualified leads: prospects who spoke with a loan officer, confirmed their age, home value, equity, and intent to move forward with a reverse mortgage."
      - "We set up a multi-stage qualification funnel with distinct conversion events at each stage. Pre-qualified leads (form fills that passed initial criteria) were tracked separately from pricing qualified leads (confirmed by the loan officer). Negative conversions were sent back to Google when leads turned out to be unqualified, teaching the algorithm who not to target."
      - "The campaign architecture used Performance Max for audience discovery at the top of funnel, Standard Search to lock in high-intent queries, and Meta Ads for retargeting and awareness among the 62+ demographic. As the account matured, the algorithm got progressively better at finding the right people because we were feeding it real business outcome data, not just form fills."
